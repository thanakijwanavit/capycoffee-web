The stall

One counter. Warm water. Clear coffee.

Capycoffee borrows Ichiran’s discipline: a short order, a ticket, a focused cup. We roast Freebird on a Kaffelogic Nano 7 — a small, repeatable machine that suits a one-SKU shop. Beans are primary. A tasting cup is an add-on if you want the first bloom in porcelain instead of a mug you already own.

Why a capybara underwater?

Because the shop should feel like a calm soak, not a tasting-room exam. Young, adult, and grandpa are just light, medium, and dark with better names. The water is teal; the cup is warm; the bird on the bag is Freebird.

Roaster

Kaffelogic Nano 7. Profiles are saved as 1.0 / 3.0 / 5.0 so the same lot tastes like itself next week. We roast to the window you pick on the ticket — not a warehouse pallet.

What we sell: Freebird beans in 150g, 250g, or 500g.

What we do not sell: a twelve-origin menu, flavored syrups, or a second rotating SKU pretending to be a catalog.

Optional: brew-method note, tasting cup sidecar.
